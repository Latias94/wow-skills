## Interpretation for This Character

H8 remains the best established route for the 321 weapon and trinkets; prior
necklace roll gives potential exclusion progress pending current preview.
The non-tier head is useful but not an automatic final-slot decision. Protect
four-piece if adopting it. Do not compare a hypothetical full pool with these
equipped-item counts: usage is not roll probability.

H7 is a conditional second-trinket source, not a mandatory whole pool to clear.
Its cloak and one-handed weapon overlap existing/planned routes, but that does
not make its passive trinket obsolete. H2 is another passive candidate and H3/
Lost Explorers another attainable alternative. A sparse desired pool can justify
waiting for a Vault drop or trade rather than spending coins immediately.

H4 is a fallback for shoulders/ring/tier progression, not a confirmed no-filler
pool. A strong shoulder item is compatible with some tier layouts, but a
non-tier head plus non-tier shoulder plus non-tier chest cannot be treated as
three simultaneous additions to a four-piece final set.
