# Ranking Targets

Use qualitative comparisons rather than multiplying invented numerical weights.

1. Current gain: weapon damage, main stats, strong effects, four-piece, and the
   actual item replaced.
2. End-state value: content/Hero-talent-matched guides or sims. Maximum raid BiS
   differs from the best obtainable Heroic-roll version; equal names do not imply
   equal levels or power.
3. Useful outcome density: distinguish long-term power, temporary upgrades,
   and achievement progress. An off-role trinket may help only the last goal.
4. Access and cost: actual coin cost, clearability, release dates, lockouts,
   time budget, and confirmed exclusion progress.
5. Alternatives and overlap: Vault, natural drops, crafting, catalyst charges,
   unique-equipped restrictions, and embellishment displacement.

Label targets as strong long-term items, plausible alternatives, temporary
upgrades, or distant maximum-level BiS. Use sims for close comparisons; without
a sim, state evidence and uncertainty rather than a personal DPS percentage.

For high-player references retain source, sample count, region, content, Hero
tree filter, and snapshot date. A popular 344 item does not prove its 334 version
wins. Population stats are not breakpoints; raid logs are not M+ benchmarks.
